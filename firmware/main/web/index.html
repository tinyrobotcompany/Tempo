First time on this iPhone?

Add the supplied Weather Station Location shortcut once. Then return here and choose Use my iPhone location. Apple will ask for location permission. You never need to edit the shortcut.

Drag the map to place your location under the centre pin.

Address lookup: IGN / Base Adresse Nationale. Map: OpenStreetMap. These services receive the address or map area you request.

Taking it with you?

On your iPhone, open Settings → Personal Hotspot. Enable Allow Others to Join and Maximise Compatibility if available. Keep that page open while scanning.

On the display, open Settings → Wi-Fi, select your iPhone and enter its hotspot password. Scan the display’s new QR code after it connects.

Update your location only if the display has moved. If your phone cannot reach the display through its own hotspot, try another phone connected to that hotspot.
